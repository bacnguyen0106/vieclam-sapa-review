Practical Tips for Your Journey

What to Pack for Mountain Weather?

Sapa experiences 4 seasons in a single day. Even in summer, evenings drop to 15°C–18°C. Pack layered clothing, a windproof lightweight jacket, sturdy walking shoes, and sunglasses for Fansipan peak.

Hanoi to Sapa Transport Options?

VIP Limousines take 5.5 hours directly via expressway. Overnight trains (8 hours) offer nostalgic sleeper berths arriving in Lao Cai early morning.

Currency & Digital Payments?

Major hotels, restaurants, and Sun World accept international credit cards and QR transfers. Carry cash (VND) for tribal craft purchases and village entry tickets.
